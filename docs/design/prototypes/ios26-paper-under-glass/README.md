# Folio iOS 26 Paper Under Glass Prototype

This folder contains the paper-material redesign direction for Folio iOS.

## Direction

**Paper under Glass**:

- content surfaces use a warm paper white base with a nearly invisible paper grain
- controls, sheets, input bars, and navigation affordances use iOS 26-style glass
- text uses ink-like contrast instead of pure stark black on pure white
- evidence, reading, and review states feel calm and trustworthy
- no retro skeuomorphism, notebook decorations, heavy card stacks, or marketing surfaces

## Color Notes

- Paper background: `#FAF8F2`
- Primary text: `#1C1C1E`
- Secondary text: `#6F6A61`
- Accent: restrained iOS blue for primary actions, progress, evidence, and send states
- Highlight: soft translucent marker yellow
